# Book: calls priced against the market

Paper positions with a size, each entered at a published market price and committed by a GitHub Actions run, so the entry carries GitHub's signature and timestamp. A call is never edited: it is marked, closed by a second file, or settled against published day-ahead prices. P&L in €/MWh is per MWh of the delivery period; P&L in € is that times the size and the hours of the period. No transaction costs are deducted.

No calls yet. A call is opened from the Actions tab with the `call (open)` workflow.
